Crispy Chicken Lolipop

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken lollipop pieces
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 medium onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1-inch piece ginger
  • 2–3 green chillies
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp red chilli powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2–3 tbsp cooking oil

Prepare the chicken

Clean the chicken lollipop pieces and pat them dry. Add the chicken, cornflour, all-purpose flour, egg, black pepper, red chilli powder, and a little salt to a bowl. Mix well until the chicken is evenly coated.

Marinate the chicken

Add soy sauce, chilli sauce, tomato ketchup, and vinegar to the chicken. Mix everything well and let it marinate for about 20–30 minutes.

Fry the chicken

Heat oil in a deep pan over medium-high heat. Carefully add the chicken lollipop pieces and fry until they become golden, crispy, and fully cooked. Remove them and place them on a plate.

Prepare the sauce

Heat 1–2 tablespoons of oil in a pan. Add chopped garlic, ginger, and green chillies. Stir-fry for a few seconds, then add soy sauce, chilli sauce, and tomato ketchup. Cook for about 1 minute.

Combine the chicken

Add the fried chicken lollipops to the pan. Toss gently over medium heat until the sauce evenly coats the chicken.

Finish and garnish

Taste and add more chilli sauce or salt if needed. Turn off the heat and garnish with chopped spring onions and sesame seeds. Serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce.

Chef's Tips

Double-fry the chicken for extra crispiness. Fry until lightly golden first, then fry again briefly until deep golden and crunchy.

Prep Time 20 min

Cook Time 20 min

Total Time 40 min
