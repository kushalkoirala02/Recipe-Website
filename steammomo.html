MOMO

Steamed Chicken Momos

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)r
  • 1 medium onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1-inch piece ginger
  • 2–3 green chillies
  • 2 tbsp spring onions
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ cup water (as needed)
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh coriander (optional)

    Prepare the dough

    Add the all-purpose flour and a little salt to a mixing bowl. Gradually add water and knead until you get a smooth and soft dough. Cover the dough and let it rest for about 20–30 minutes.

    Prepare the chicken filling

    Finely chop or mince the boneless chicken. Add the chicken, chopped onion, garlic, ginger, green chillies, spring onions, black pepper, cumin powder, soy sauce, sesame oil, and salt to a bowl. Mix everything well until the filling is evenly combined.

    Roll the wrappers

    Divide the dough into small, equal-sized portions. Roll each portion into a thin, round wrapper. Keep the edges slightly thinner than the center so the momos are easy to fold.

    Fill the momos

    Place about 1 tablespoon of chicken filling in the center of each wrapper. Fold the wrapper over the filling and pinch the edges together to seal it completely. Repeat with the remaining wrappers and filling.

    Prepare the steamer

    Bring water to a gentle boil in a steamer. Lightly grease the steaming tray with cooking oil to prevent the momos from sticking.

    Steam the momos

    Arrange the momos on the greased steamer tray, leaving a little space between each one. Cover and steam for about 12–15 minutes, or until the wrappers become slightly translucent and the chicken is fully cooked.

    Chef's Tips

    Don’t overfill the momos, and seal the edges tightly. Lightly grease the steamer tray to prevent sticking and keep the momos soft.

    Cook Time 15 min

    Total Time 35 min
