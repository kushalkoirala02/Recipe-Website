Crispy Chilli Chicken

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 medium onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1-inch piece ginger
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 2–3 tbsp cooking oil
  • 2 tbsp spring onions (optional)
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds (optional)

Prepare the chicken

Cut the boneless chicken into small, bite-sized pieces. Add the chicken, cornflour, egg, black pepper, and a little salt to a bowl. Mix well until every piece is evenly coated.

Fry the chicken

Heat oil in a deep pan over medium-high heat. Carefully add the coated chicken pieces and fry until they become golden, crispy, and fully cooked. Remove them and place them on a plate.

Prepare the vegetables

Heat 1–2 tablespoons of oil in a large pan or wok. Add the chopped garlic and ginger and stir-fry for a few seconds. Add the onion, bell pepper, and green chillies and cook for about 2–3 minutes. Keep the vegetables slightly crunchy.

Make the sauce

Add the soy sauce, chilli sauce, tomato ketchup, and vinegar to the pan. Stir everything together and cook for about 1 minute until the sauce becomes hot and well combined.

Combine the chicken

Add the fried chicken to the pan. Toss everything together over medium-high heat until the sauce evenly coats all the chicken pieces.

Finish and garnish

Taste and add more salt or chilli sauce if needed. Turn off the heat and garnish with chopped spring onions and sesame seeds.

Chef's Tips

Double fry the chicken for extra crispiness first until lightly golden, then fry it again briefly until deep golden and crunchy. This keeps the chicken crispy even after tossing it in the sauce.

Prep Time 15 min

Cook Time 15 min

Total Time 30 min
