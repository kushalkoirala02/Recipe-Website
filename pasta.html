Midnight Tomato Bucatini

Ingredients

  • 250 g bucatini pasta
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 medium onion
  • 400 g canned crushed tomatoes
  • ½ tsp red chilli flakes
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ cup pasta water/li>
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp fresh basil leaves (optional)
  • 1 tbsp butter (optional)

Cook the pasta

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the bucatini pasta and cook according to the package instructions until al dente. Before draining, reserve about ½ cup of pasta water.

Prepare the sauce

Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the finely chopped onion and cook for 2–3 minutes until softened. Add the minced garlic and cook for another 30 seconds until fragrant.

Build the tomato sauce

Add the tomato paste and stir for about 1 minute. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, then add the chilli flakes, black pepper, oregano, basil, sugar, and a little salt. Stir everything together and let the sauce simmer for 8–10 minutes until it becomes rich and slightly thick.

Combine the pasta

Add the cooked bucatini directly into the tomato sauce. Toss well so the sauce coats every strand of pasta. Add a little reserved pasta water if the sauce feels too thick.

Finish the pasta

Add the butter and grated Parmesan cheese. Toss everything together over low heat until the cheese melts and the sauce becomes smooth and glossy.

Garnish and serve

Taste and adjust the salt or chilli flakes if needed. Turn off the heat and garnish with fresh basil and extra Parmesan. Serve the Midnight Tomato Bucatini while hot.

Chef's Tips

Double fry the chicken for extra crispiness first until lightly golden, then fry it again briefly until deep golden and crunchy. This keeps the chicken crispy even after tossing it in the sauce.
