Savory Chicken Chowmein

Ingredients

  • 250 g chowmein noodles
  • 300 g boneless chicken, thinly sliced
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 1 medium carrot, julienned
  • 1 medium capsicum, sliced
  • ½ cup cabbage, shredded
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1-inch piece ginger, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2–3 tbsp cooking oil

Cook the noodles

Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add a little salt and cook the chowmein noodles according to the package instructions until just tender. Drain and rinse with cold water to stop the cooking process. Toss with a little oil and set aside.

Prepare the chicken

Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large wok or pan over medium-high heat. Add the sliced chicken, black pepper, and a little salt. Stir-fry for 4–5 minutes until the chicken is fully cooked and lightly browned. Remove and set aside.

Prepare the vegetables

Add the soy sauce, chilli sauce, tomato ketchup, and vinegar. Stir everything together and cook for about 1 minute until the sauce is hot and well combined.

Make the sauce

Add the soy sauce, chilli sauce, tomato ketchup, and vinegar to the pan. Stir everything together and cook for about 1 minute until the sauce becomes hot and well combined.

Combine everything

Add the cooked noodles and chicken to the wok. Toss everything together over high heat for 2–3 minutes until the noodles are evenly coated with the sauce and all the ingredients are well combined.

Finish and garnish

Taste and add more salt or chilli sauce if needed. Turn off the heat and garnish with chopped spring onions and sesame seeds.

Chef's Tips

Do not overcook the noodles, as they can become soft and mushy when stir-fried. Cook the vegetables over high heat for a short time to keep them slightly crunchy. For extra smoky flavour, toss the noodles quickly over high heat before serving.

Prep Time 15 min

Cook Time 15 min

Total Time 30 min
