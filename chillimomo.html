Spicy Chilli Momo

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken momo
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1-inch piece ginger, minced
  • 3–4 green chillies, finely chopped
  • 1 medium capsicum, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2–3 tbsp cooking oil
  • 2 tbsp spring onions (optional)
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds (optional)

Prepare the momo filling

Finely chop the chicken, onion, garlic, ginger, and green chillies. Add them to a bowl along with soy sauce, black pepper, cumin powder, and salt. Mix everything well until the filling is evenly combined.

Prepare the momo dough

Add all-purpose flour and a little salt to a bowl. Gradually add water and knead until you get a smooth, soft dough. Cover and let it rest for about 15–20 minutes.

Shape the momos

Divide the dough into small portions and roll each portion into a thin circle. Place a spoonful of the chicken filling in the center. Fold and seal the edges carefully to form the momo shape.

Steam the momos

Place the momos in a greased steamer, leaving some space between them. Steam for about 12–15 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked and the momo wrappers become soft and slightly translucent.

Prepare the spicy chilli sauce

Heat 1–2 tablespoons of oil in a large pan. Add chopped garlic, ginger, green chillies, and capsicum. Stir-fry for 2–3 minutes while keeping the vegetables slightly crunchy.

Toss the momos

Add the steamed momos to the pan. Gently toss them over medium-high heat until the spicy sauce evenly coats all the momos.

Chef's Tips

For extra flavour and crispiness, lightly pan-fry the steamed momos before adding them to the chilli sauce. Toss them gently so the momos stay intact while getting evenly coated with the spicy sauce.

Prep Time 20 min

Cook Time 25 min

Total Time 45 min
