About the chef

Marco Alvani

I cooked in restaurants for fifteen years before I understood that the

most useful thing a chef can give a home cook isn't a recipe — it's a

reason.

Table & Ember is where I write those reasons down. Why the pan has to be

that hot. Why the salt goes in early. Why the sauce wants starchy water and

not cream. Everything here is cooked in a normal kitchen, with normal

equipment, on the kind of evening where you're already tired.

If a recipe needs a tool you don't have, I'll tell you what to use instead. If it

needs patience, I'll tell you that too.

2009

First line cook job, a 22-seat trattoria in Bologna. Learned pasta by repetition.

2014

Sous chef in Copenhagen. Discovered that restraint is a technique too.

2019

Opened a neighbourhood kitchen focused on vegetables and fire.

2023

Started Table & Ember to write down what the kitchen taught me.
