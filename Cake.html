Cake

Classic Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup cocoa powder
  • 1½ cups granulated sugar
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • 1½ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup hot water
  • ½ cup chocolate chips (optional)

Prepare the dry ingredients

Preheat the oven to 180°C. In a large bowl, sift together the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Mix well and set aside.

Prepare the wet ingredients

In another bowl, whisk together the eggs, sugar, milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract until the mixture becomes smooth and well combined.

Combine the ingredients

Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture. Gently mix until everything is combined and there are no large lumps. Do not overmix the batter.

Add the hot water

Slowly pour in the hot water while stirring gently. The batter will become thin, which is normal and helps make the cake soft and moist. Add chocolate chips if desired.

Bake the cake

Pour the batter into a greased and lined cake pan. Bake at 180°C for about 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

Cool and decorate

Remove the cake from the oven and let it cool in the pan for about 10 minutes. Transfer it to a wire rack and allow it to cool completely before slicing. Decorate with chocolate frosting or a light dusting of cocoa powder if desired.

Chef's Tips

Do not overmix the batter after adding the flour, as this can make the cake dense. Make sure the oven is fully preheated before baking, and allow the cake to cool completely before frosting for the best results.

Prep Time 15 min

Cook Time 35 min

Total Time 50 min
